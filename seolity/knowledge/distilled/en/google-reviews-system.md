# Write high quality reviews

Google's guidance on writing review content that helps people make decisions about products, services and other topics. It stresses first-hand expertise, evidence, original research and usefulness over length, which matters for review pages and WooCommerce buying guides to be discovered in Search and other Google surfaces.

## Demonstrate expertise and first-hand experience

- Do evaluate the subject from the user's perspective, because reviews should help readers decide.
- Do show that you are knowledgeable about what you review, since demonstrated expertise makes the review more credible.
- Do provide evidence of your own experience, such as visuals, audio or links, because it supports your expertise and reinforces the authenticity of the review.
- Do base the benefits and drawbacks you discuss on your own original research rather than repeating generic claims.

## Provide substantive, decision-focused analysis

- Do share quantitative measurements of how the item performs in various categories, because measurable data helps readers compare.
- Do explain what sets the item apart from its competitors.
- Do cover comparable alternatives, or explain which option is best for certain uses or circumstances.
- Do focus on the most important decision-making factors based on your experience (for example, a car review might identify fuel economy and safety as key factors and rate performance in those areas).
- Do describe key design choices and their effect on users beyond what the manufacturer says.
- Do describe how a product has evolved from previous models or releases, such as improvements or fixed issues, when that helps a purchase decision.

## Best-of and ranked lists

- Do explain why you consider something the best overall or best for a specific purpose, and back it with first-hand supporting evidence.
- Do make sure ranked lists contain enough useful content to stand on their own, even if you also write separate in-depth single reviews.

## Helpful links and affiliate content

- Do include links to other useful resources, your own or from other sites, to help readers make a decision.
- Consider linking to multiple sellers so readers can buy from the merchant of their choice.
- If your reviews use affiliate links, do review Google's position on affiliate programs, because reviewers can be rewarded by the seller when readers purchase.

## Prioritize quality over length

- Do focus on the quality and originality of reviews rather than their length, because this delivers the most value to readers.
- Do follow as many of the listed best practices as you are able on each review page.
