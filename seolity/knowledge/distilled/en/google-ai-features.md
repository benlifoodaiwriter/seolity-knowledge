# AI features (AI Overviews and AI Mode) and your website

Google states that the standard SEO best practices apply to AI Overviews and AI Mode, with no extra technical requirements or special markup. This guidance explains eligibility, which fundamentals to maintain, how to measure performance, and how to control how content appears.

## How AI features work

- Do treat AI Overviews and AI Mode as part of Google Search that surface supporting links, not as a separate channel needing its own optimization approach, because Google says SEO best practices remain relevant.
- Do expect AI Overviews to appear only on some queries: they are shown when Google's systems determine they add value beyond classic Search, so they often don't trigger.
- Do create content that serves complex, exploratory or comparison-style questions, because AI Mode is designed for queries that need exploration, reasoning or complex comparisons.
- Do cover related subtopics of a subject, because both features may use a 'query fan-out' technique that issues multiple related searches across subtopics and data sources.
- Do not assume AI Overviews and AI Mode show the same links, because they may use different models and techniques.

## Eligibility and technical requirements

- Do make sure a page is indexed and eligible to be shown in Google Search with a snippet, because that is the requirement to be a supporting link in AI Overviews or AI Mode.
- Do not look for additional technical requirements for AI features, because Google says there are none.
- Do not expect guaranteed crawling, indexing or serving even when requirements, best practices and policies are met.
- Do follow Google Search policies and create helpful, reliable, people-first content as the foundation for inclusion.

## SEO fundamentals to maintain

- Do allow crawling in robots.txt and in any CDN or hosting infrastructure, so Googlebot can reach important pages.
- Do make content easy to find through internal links on your website.
- Do provide a great page experience for users.
- Do keep important content available in textual form.
- Do support text with high-quality images and videos when applicable.
- Do make sure structured data matches the visible text on the page.
- Do keep Merchant Center and Business Profile information up to date.
- Avoid creating special machine-readable files, AI text files or extra markup for AI features, because none are needed, and no special schema.org structured data is required.
- Do verify your site in Search Console to discover and diagnose technical issues quickly.

## Measuring performance

- Do use the Search Console Performance report, 'Web' search type, to review traffic from AI features, because AI Overviews and AI Mode are included in overall search traffic there.
- Do combine Search Console data with Google Analytics to track conversions and time spent on site.
- Do consider engagement quality when evaluating AI Overview traffic, because Google reports that clicks from results pages with AI Overviews tend to be higher quality (users more likely to spend more time on the site).

## Controlling content in AI features

- Use robots.txt directives for Googlebot to manage how your site is crawled for Search, because AI is built into Search and this is the control for crawl access.
- To limit the information shown from your pages in Search, use nosnippet, data-nosnippet, max-snippet or noindex controls.
- To limit AI training and grounding in some of Google's other systems, review Google-Extended.
- If content still appears after you add preview controls, confirm the control is correct and visible to Googlebot by using the URL Inspection tool to view the HTML Googlebot received.
- Allow time for recrawling after changing preview controls, since crawling can take from several days to several months, and request a recrawl of changed pages if needed.
