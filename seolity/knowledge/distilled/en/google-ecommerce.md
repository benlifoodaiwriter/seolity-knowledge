# Best practices for ecommerce sites in Google Search (overview)

This overview describes Google's guide set for making ecommerce sites discoverable in Google Search and other Google surfaces. Sharing product data and a clear site structure helps Google find and parse content, which helps shoppers find your products. The page lists the main areas to cover: surfaces, product data, structured data, launch, reviews, URL design, site structure and pagination.

## Product data and visibility

- Do share your ecommerce data and site structure with Google, because this makes it easier for Google to find and parse your content so it can appear in Google Search and other Google surfaces.
- Do review the different surfaces where ecommerce content can appear on Google, so you know where your products can be shown.
- Do decide deliberately which method to use for sharing product data with Google, rather than leaving it to chance.
- Apply these practices to sites that list products available only in brick and mortar stores as well, because many of the points are equally relevant to them.

## Structured data

- Do include structured data relevant to ecommerce, because it gives Google explicit information about the meaning of the page and helps Google understand and appropriately present your content.

## Launching a new site

- Do plan the launch of a new ecommerce website strategically, including the timing of when you register the site with Google.

## Reviews

- Do write high quality product reviews that help shoppers decide on the right product for their needs.

## URL structure and site architecture

- Do design the URL structure of an ecommerce site with care to avoid crawling and URL design issues that are specific to ecommerce sites.
- Do design a clear site navigation structure and link between pages so Google can understand what is most important on your ecommerce site.

## Pagination and page loading

- Do consider how UX patterns such as pagination and incremental page loading affect Google's ability to crawl and index your content, and choose patterns that keep your content crawlable and indexable.
